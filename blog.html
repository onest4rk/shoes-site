How to choose the right running shoe

Start with your stride, the terrain, and the amount of cushioning you want underfoot. A runner should feel secure without pressure on the top of the foot.

Care guide • 4 min read

Caring for mesh, leather, and suede

Use a soft brush for loose dirt, keep the materials dry while cleaning, and let shoes air out naturally after wear. Heat is the fastest way to damage the finish.

Why rotating your shoes matters

Giving foam a day to recover can help it keep its feel longer. It also keeps pairs cleaner, drier, and easier to inspect for wear.
